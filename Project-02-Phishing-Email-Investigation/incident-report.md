# Phishing Email – Incident Report

## 1. Incident Summary

An employee received a suspicious email claiming that their company account would be suspended unless they completed urgent account verification.

The email contained a suspicious URL and an unexpected HTML attachment.

## 2. Email Details

- Sender: it-support@example.invalid
- Reply-To: account-help@unknown.invalid
- Subject: Urgent: Your company account will be suspended!
- Suspicious URL: hxxps://company-login.example.invalid/verify
- Attachment: Account_Verification.html

## 3. Investigation Findings

The following suspicious indicators were identified:

1. Urgent and threatening language.
2. Unverified sender identity.
3. Mismatch between Sender and Reply-To addresses.
4. Request for credential verification through a link.
5. Unexpected HTML attachment.

## 4. Risk Assessment

- Classification: Suspected Phishing
- Initial Severity: High – Pending Investigation
- Potential Impact: Credential theft and unauthorized account access.

## 5. Recommended Response

1. Do not click the suspicious URL.
2. Do not open the attachment.
3. Report and escalate the email to the security team.
4. Preserve the original email and headers.
5. Search for similar emails across the organization.
6. If credentials were submitted, initiate the organization's account-compromise response process.

## 6. Conclusion

The email contains multiple phishing indicators. It should be treated as suspected phishing and escalated for further investigation.

No actual compromise has been confirmed in this simulated scenario.

**Note:** This is a fictional educational SOC investigation. All domains and indicators are intentionally non-functional.
